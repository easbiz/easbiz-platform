# EASBIZ

### Making Business Easier

EASBIZ is a global AI Business Operating Platform designed to help businesses create, manage and supervise AI employees.

## Our Vision

To make intelligent business operations accessible to businesses worldwide.

## Core Platform Features

1. AI Workforce Manager
2. AI Employee Creation and Management
3. Business Workflow Automation
4. Customer Operations Management
5. Human Approval and Security Controls
6. Activity Logs and Performance Analytics
7. Business Application Integrations

## Development Strategy

Build a scalable, secure, subscription-based software platform.

The initial MVP will focus on AI employee management and customer operations.

## Project Status

Foundation and planning stage.
